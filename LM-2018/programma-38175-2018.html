Learning outcomes
Knowledge

The student who successfully completes the course will know the following topics:

  • The discrete symmetries CPT in Elementary particles and the conservation laws.
  • The first particle physics experiments that detected symmetry violations of Parity P,Charge Conjugation C and their product CP.
  • Elementary weak interactions and an introduction to neutrino oscillations.
  • Hadronic physics measurements at e+e- Colliders.
  • The discovery of quark c and elementary QCD model.
Assessment criteria of knowledge

- In the written exam (3 hours, 2 exercises each with 3 or 4 questions), the student must demonstrate his/her knowledge of the course material, its capability to organize an effective and correctly written reply. - With his/her oral presentation to the teachers, the student must demonstrate the ability to approach a circumscribed research problem, and organize an effective exposition of the results.

Methods:

  • Final oral exam
  • Final written exam

Further information:
The student is strongly advised to pass the written test before admission to the final oral exam. At the link: https://elearning.df.unipi.it/course/view.php?id=312 interested students will find texts and solutions of the previous years written tests and several additional scientific and historical papers and references.
Prerequisites

Classical, relativistic and quantum physics of the first three year Physics course.

Syllabus

The discrete symmetries CPT in Elementary particles, the conservation laws, the first experiments that detected symmetry violations of P,C and CP. Elementary weak interactions. Hadronic physics measurements at e+e- Colliders. Charm quark discovery and elementary QCD model.
